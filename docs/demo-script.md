## Before recording

- Start a clean browser session and call the public reset endpoint through the interface.
- Confirm the active demo session shows Scene 12 with `Blue jacket`.
- Confirm the displayed Runtime proof identifies the official `mcp-clickhouse` version and sanitized correlation ID, without SQL, URLs, or secrets.
- Have one pre-captured `Review required` screen ready for the uncertainty proof at the end.

## 0:00–0:20 — the consequence

**Say:** “Creative changes are normal. The risk is when a change silently reaches footage already shot or tomorrow’s call sheet. SlateGuard gives a script supervisor one evidence-backed decision loop.”

**Show:** Scene 12. Point once to the current `Blue jacket` fact and the single `Apply revision` control.

## 0:20–0:50 — make the revision

**Say:** “I’m changing Scene 12 from blue jacket to black jacket.”

**Show:** Apply the prepared revision. Do not narrate implementation details yet. Keep the evidence trace in view as it shows the revision write and reader retrieval.

## 0:50–1:25 — prove the blast radius

**Say:** “The product retrieves the production record first. Scene 11 was already filmed with blue. Scenes 13 and 14 are on tomorrow’s call sheet with the same continuity requirement.”

**Show:** The three evidence cards, their source IDs, and the impact strip: `1 continuity conflict · 2 scenes on tomorrow's call sheet · Wardrobe + AD review required`.

## 1:25–1:50 — explain the agent boundary

**Say:** “The model does not decide the risk. Deterministic rules produce these findings; Gemini only turns the bounded facts and source IDs into this concise Change Packet.”

**Show:** The grounded Change Packet and its citations. Do not show prompts or raw model output.

## 1:50–2:20 — make the human decision

**Say:** “SlateGuard does not automatically create work. The script supervisor explicitly creates a follow-up for Wardrobe and the Assistant Director.”

**Show:** Select `Create follow-up`. Hold long enough to show writer persistence, reader verification, action ID, owners, and `At risk → Follow-up created`.

## 2:20–2:40 — make the sponsor proof visible

**Say:** “Every production-memory read and write in this loop runs through separate least-privilege instances of the official ClickHouse MCP server. The trace shows the write and independent reader verification without exposing credentials or SQL.”

**Show:** Expand Runtime proof for one uninterrupted view: package version, sanitized correlation ID, write/read status, action ID, and reader-confirmed readiness.

## 2:40–2:55 — prove restraint

**Say:** “When sources are missing or contradict one another, SlateGuard does not fabricate certainty. It switches to Review required and withholds the follow-up.”

**Show:** The pre-captured abstention screen briefly.

## 2:55–3:00 — close

**Say:** “SlateGuard turns a creative revision into an accountable, evidence-first production decision.”

**Show:** Return to the decision receipt or proof map. End on the action ID and visible reader verification, not a logo screen.
